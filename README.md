## Stage rules this deck follows

Built for a darkened hall and a 16:9 screen, readable from the back row.

1. **One idea in the picture.** The sentence is spoken. Speaker notes (`S`) carry the script, the timing, and the open TODOs from the outline.
2. **1920×1080, full bleed.** Body type stays at or above 24px in that coordinate space. Fade only. No slide-to-slide trick transitions.
3. **Contrast for a projector.** Charcoal field, warm paper for the emails, the loan quote, Monday and the close, violet when the section turns. No pure black or white.
4. **Color is never the only signal.** Intent, constraints and acceptance keep their words. Crossed stations are struck through. The Done stamp is a word, not a green dot.
5. **Numbers stay attributed on the slide.** 11.5 → 6.7, 44% / 48%, and 51% are labeled as Atlassian’s figures.
6. **Motion in two places.** The harness draws itself on the title, and the code segment of the pipeline squeezes. Everything else is still.
7. **Graphics, not forged screenshots.** Emails, the investigation, the PR fan and `AGENTS.md` are drawn in the deck’s type. Swap in redacted screenshots when you have consent and a crop that stays readable at the back of the room.
8. **The path is the right arrow.** If you are behind at code.talks: at 17:00 go to Same Model. Different System.
